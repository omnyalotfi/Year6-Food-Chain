YEAR 6 · SCIENCE · 5-MINUTE TEAM CHALLENGE

🌿 Food Chain Challenge

Work with your two teammates in your assigned breakout room. Create a food chain, think like scientists and get ready to share your ideas.

05:00

Suggested timing: 1 minute to organise, 2 minutes to create, 1 minute to investigate, 1 minute to share.

1. Join your team

Choose your room to see your ecosystem prompt.

2. Create your food chain

As a team, choose at least four organisms and put them in the correct feeding order. Use arrows to show the direction of energy transfer.

3. Think like a scientist!

If one organism in your food chain disappeared, what might happen to the other organisms?

4. Get ready to share

Choose one speaker. In the main room, explain your food chain and share your prediction in about 20 seconds.

This page does not send answers to your teacher automatically. After completing the activity, download your answers and share the file with your teacher using the class platform. Your teacher can publish this HTML file as a webpage to give everyone one shared link.
